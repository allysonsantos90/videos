HOW IT WORKS

A considered process.

Clear communication before the event helps create a relaxed experience and a film that reflects what matters most to you.

01 · Get in touch

Tell me about your event, date, location and the kind of film you have in mind.

02 · Plan the story

We discuss the schedule, priorities, important people and the moments you would like captured.

03 · Filming

On the day, I focus on genuine moments, atmosphere, details and the natural flow of the celebration.

04 · Editing & delivery

Your footage is shaped into a finished film and delivered digitally, with any agreed shorter edits.
