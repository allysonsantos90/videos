AS VISUAL FILMS — WEBSITE STARTER

Files
- index.html: page content and sections
- styles.css: design, layout and responsive styles
- script.js: mobile navigation and video-link settings

PREVIEW
Open index.html in a browser. The design uses Google Fonts and remote Unsplash placeholder photographs, so an internet connection is needed for those assets.

PERSONALISE BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. In index.html, replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your email address.
2. Replace the Instagram and YouTube href="#" links with your profile URLs.
3. In script.js, add the YouTube/Vimeo links for:
   preview, getting-ready, ceremony, vows, reception.
4. Replace placeholder stock photos with still frames from your own wedding films. The current images are illustrative and are not photos from Craig & Sophie's wedding.
5. Confirm you have permission to publish identifiable wedding footage and music.
