THE PERSON BEHIND THE CAMERA

Allyson Santos

Filmmaker & Visual Storyteller · Based in Ireland

I believe every story deserves to be seen from a different perspective. From the beauty of nature and everyday life to the emotions of weddings and meaningful celebrations, my approach to filmmaking is driven by storytelling, creativity and attention to detail.

AS Visual Stories brings these two sides of my work together: personal films shaped by curiosity, and client films created to preserve meaningful moments.
